PartForge Altium bridge - third-party notices
================================================

The DelphiScript units in this folder (PFUtils.pas, PFLibraries.pas, PFSchLib.pas,
PFPcbLib.pas, PartForgeBridge.pas) were written for PartForge. Parts of their design
- the request/response exchange through files, the X2.EXE RunScript launch, the
spec-file driven construction of schematic symbols and PCB footprints and the
nil-safe access to the PCB/SCH servers - are derived from altium-mcp:

    altium-mcp - https://github.com/coffeenmusic/altium-mcp
